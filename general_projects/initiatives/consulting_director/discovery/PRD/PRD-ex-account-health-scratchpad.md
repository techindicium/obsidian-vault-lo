## Discovery Answers — Slack Q&A with PP (pre-meeting, 08/03/2026)

Before the live discovery meeting, Lorena asked PP a few direct questions on Slack to clarify the product's objective (Step 3 / JTBD). These answers are captured here as raw findings; they still need to be folded into `PRD-ex-account-health.md` Section 2 (Objective and JTBD) once the full discovery is done.

**Question asked:** "Is the goal of this initiative just to save time on filling out reports, or also to spot account risk earlier, have more leverage in a conversation with leadership, generate an automatic report for Quadros and Fernando, etc.?"

**PP's answer — two main objectives, not just time savings:**

1. **Spot opportunities and keep commercial speed.** This is not only about faster data entry — it also means actively pushing the sales process forward: following up on next steps, preparing and reviewing proposals. So the tool should support *action*, not just visibility.
2. **Spot risk early.** Catch problems before they "sneak up" on someone (this echoes the earlier question about why this initiative is happening now).

PP also said these two objectives can be broken down into better indicators later — meaning the panel's metrics should be designed backward from these two goals, not just copied from the fields that already exist in today's spreadsheet.

**"Account planning" clarified:** each account has a specific revenue target (example given: Bayer has a R$5MM/year potential). "Adherence to account planning" means comparing the account's real progress against that target, and answering questions like:
- Do we have enough opportunities in the CRM to hit that target?
- Are we pacing faster or slower than expected?
- Is anything on the delivery side putting new opportunities at risk?

**Where the target number comes from:** it's set jointly by the **CD and the Account Executive**, but ownership of the number sits with the **Commercial team**, not with Delivery/CDs. The number itself is "a bit of all three": a calculation, a speculation, and something partly known from the client (e.g., the client's annual AI budget). This means the target is not a hard number pulled from a system today — it is a judgment call, and it is not clear yet whether it's tracked consistently anywhere across all 8 CDs, or only in each CD's head.

→ **This is a new data dependency, separate from R1** (Profitability/GM% owned by Ops/Financeiro): the account target lives with the **Commercial org**, which means a *second, independent* data-access conversation is needed. See R7 below.

**Where notes/risks/opportunities come from today:** meetings. PP confirmed this is "nothing structured" — he pulls it from the transcripts of recurring EX meetings. This directly confirms R2 (unstructured source data) with a concrete example.

**Technical precedent worth noting for the Design phase:** PP mentioned he already uses some informal Claude-based automation that filters the `[EX] Monitoring` spreadsheet down to only the accounts assigned to him, to build his personal draft. This is not a finished system, but it's evidence that a similar approach (LLM + spreadsheet filtering) already works informally for at least one CD.

**Confirmed:** PP's prototype is a personal view, scoped to his own accounts — not a shared multi-CD view. This matches the existing R4 concern (solution designed around one CD's habits).

**Account-planning spreadsheet reviewed (structure only — no client names or figures kept in this file):** PP shared a copy of the official account-planning spreadsheet so Lorena could see how tracking is actually done today. Lorena confirmed we can pull data from the original spreadsheet — this is a real, structured system, not a mockup. Structure found:
- A portfolio-level overview: one row per account, with columns for client name, Consulting Director, Account Executive, status, a relevance tier (A/B/C), industry, and the year's potential revenue.
- A detail block per account (repeated for every account in the portfolio), with the same header fields plus: potential revenue for the year (the target number) broken down month by month, a "weighted revenue" field (present but empty in the copy reviewed — likely meant to hold probability-weighted revenue), and the client's tech stack.
- Below that, an engagement-level table: one row per active opportunity/project under that account, with fields for engagement name, solution line, probability (a mix of qualitative status labels and numeric percentages), engagement model, billing format, hourly rate, hours planned per month (Jan–Dec), and computed total hours/revenue for the year.

This resolves R7 below: the account target is **not** unstructured or only "in someone's head" — it lives in a real, granular, already-structured spreadsheet, access to the original is confirmed as feasible, and Lorena confirmed all 8 CDs use this same spreadsheet (08/03/2026).

## Discovery Interview with Onohara — Cross-CD Generalization Check (08/13/2026)

Second CD interview (see `meetings/Lorena  Onohara - EX Account Health - 2026_08_13 - Resumo e Próximos Passos.md` for the full narrative and quotes), run against `discovery_ex_account_health_other_cds.md`. Onohara manages only 3 accounts; the mock/prototype was shown only **after** pure pain-collection was done, so the process/pain findings (questions 1–4) are not contaminated by the mock, while the report-content reactions (question 5) are genuine solution-validation feedback, not pain discovery.

**Process/pain findings:**
- Much less structured process than PP — Onohara keeps most tracking "in his head," attributes this partly to having few accounts.
- Real difficulty is lack of forward-looking visibility (not judgment): financial/billing flow, people allocation (vacation clashing with fixed-scope project timeboxes), and CRM data that's incomplete for cultural reasons, not tooling reasons.
- Heavy, confident daily AI usage already (scenario simulation, structuring thinking before client meetings, fast presentation prep) — a more mature AI habit than PP's.
- Wants AI monitoring market/Slack asynchronously to generate "already-chewed" commercial provocations — not available today.
- "If this were easier" answer: automated financial anticipation, cross-referencing active contracts with due dates, fed live by Finance.

**Report-content reactions (post-pain-collection, solution validation):** open-account counts dismissed as noise ("vou direto pros highlights"); highlights and RAG-history validated; pipeline validated with a data-quality caveat; **two new candidate items surfaced**: a financial-consumption view (billed-to-date vs. total contract value) and a per-proposal client contact/stakeholder tag (not just the assigned squad); narrative-from-transcripts validated only as reinforcement, not as new signal; **a third new candidate**: an async channel for field teams to flag incipient client discussions before they become formal opportunities; risk module (profitability, team overload, client leadership change) validated enthusiastically.

**Comparison against PP's four points (see `discovery_ex_account_health_other_cds.md`):**
- "Each CD has their own way" — **confirmed**; Onohara even proposed tooling standardization across CDs on his own.
- AI skepticism (transcript-based) vs. enthusiasm (external context) — **did not replicate as the same split**. Onohara validated transcript-based narrative (no skepticism) and drew his real line at "info lost today for lack of capture," not "internal vs. external."
- Live artifact over Slack alert — **did not generalize**. Onohara wants an actively-accessed artifact *and* a Slack nudge to build the habit — PP's alert-aversion looks personal, not CD-wide.
- Account-plan adherence as not-CD-alone — **confirmed and reinforced**; Onohara independently said this shouldn't be a single-owner judgment, converging with PP's own downgrade.

**Out-of-scope pain point, explicitly flagged (08/18/2026):** Onohara also described CDs getting pulled into operational firefighting outside their real scope — unblocking access requests, payment-flow blockers, labor-compliance issues on at least one account described as "operacionalmente um caos." **Lorena's call: this does NOT belong in this product.** It's a process/internal-responsibility-assignment problem (who should actually own these tasks inside the org), not a data-visibility problem this initiative can fix — removed from the PRD's Pain Points (Context and Motivation) accordingly, kept here only as a discovery finding.

**New organizational context surfaced (not requirements, just findings to keep in mind):** a chunk of CD operational load comes from internal teams' still-developing client-facing maturity (example: a team structuring a solution before validating the problem with the client stakeholder); one of Onohara's accounts restricts the delivery team to the client's own virtual environment, blocking Indicium's internal access to meeting recordings there — a hard technical limit for any transcript-based AI feature on that specific account; a separate, parallel Operations initiative is already working on opening profitability/margin data to CDs, still pending internal regulatory alignment — check before duplicating or conflicting with it.

**Net effect on R4 (generalization):** still open — see updated Open Question 6 and R4 above. Two of four comparison points held; two did not. Enough signal to treat PP's personal preferences (alert-aversion, transcript-skepticism split) as *his*, not universal, but not enough coverage (2/8 CDs) to close the risk.

## Possible Products — Solution Space Being Explored (08/05/2026, narrowed 08/18/2026)

**This section is reserved for candidate end-product shapes for EX Account Health itself** — not for existing tools that merely inspired reuse for a different, out-of-scope initiative, and not for upstream systems this product depends on as a data source (those belong in "Data Sources — Consolidated" below). **Correction (08/18/2026):** two earlier entries were removed on that basis — Indicium.AI (relevant only to a separate, out-of-scope proposal-review/creation idea — see Roadmap's Backlog section) and Delivery Dashboard (not a candidate product, but a confirmed upstream data source — see Pre-Discovery Synthesis and Data Sources below). One real candidate direction remains:

### Automated Slack report — one possibility explored in depth

The most-explored possibility so far, but still just that — a possibility, not a decision. If pursued, EX Account Health would be reframed as a **personal support tool for each CD** — not a leadership-reporting tool — surfaced periodically via Slack (see "Report content — final list" below for exact contents, drafted for this possibility specifically), that:
- Synthesizes RAG/delivery health from Delivery Dashboard + account-plan adherence from the Commercial account-planning spreadsheet.
- Links back into Delivery Dashboard's write-back form for RAG overrides.
- Uses Bitrix as a provisional data source if Kantata delivery data isn't ready in time (accepted by Lorena as fine to use meanwhile).
- Commercial team access/involvement to be aligned directly with PP and Commercial — Commercial may end up being an additional, separate consumer of a report.

**Delivery structure — decided at the "what Slack does" level (08/07 + confirmed/narrowed 08/18/2026):** "delivered via Slack" does **not** mean the full content renders as a Slack message, and Slack is **not** a rich front-end. Slack's only job is to notify the CD that the report is updated and hand them a link to open it — a thin notification + trigger layer, nothing more. **Where the actual artifact lives/gets generated is still an open architecture question, not yet decided (08/18/2026):**
- **Option A:** a persistent app (the two-tab Portfolio + Account Detail structure from the mock) that Slack simply links to.
- **Option B:** a Claude skill, invoked on demand — Slack notifies the CD and asks if they want to run the skill to (re)generate the artifact in Claude. If this option is chosen, the generated artifact would have the same structure/content as Option A (same two-tab report), just produced on demand rather than kept persistently updated.
