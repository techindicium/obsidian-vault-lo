## Pre-Discovery Synthesis (Step 2)

Source material analyzed: `consulting_director/initial_context/core_problems.md` (Issue 1).

**Already known (do not re-ask in discovery):**

- **Impact:** delayed commission payouts and rework for the Finance team.
- **Root cause:** required fields in Salesforce are left blank when an opportunity is created/updated, so Finance doesn't have what it needs to calculate and pay commissions correctly or on time.
- Some of the fields involved were added to the CRM recently, specifically to make this process easier — the problem now is reinforcing correct usage, not missing functionality.
- **Correct fill-in process (already documented/known):**
  1. Open the opportunity in Salesforce.
  2. Go to the "Sales Architect" tab.
  3. Find the "Opportunity Team (Commissioned)" section.
  4. Fill in the full name of everyone involved in the sale who should be commissioned, in the correct role (SA, CD, Expert).
  5. This must happen **before** the opportunity is marked as Won, since the handoff to Finance is triggered automatically at that moment.
- **How to self-check today:** create a Salesforce list view scoped to "My Opportunity Teams" (Opportunities → gear icon → New → Save → filter icon → Filter by My Opportunity Teams → Save) to see which of your own opportunities are missing this data.

**Explicit gaps — nothing flagged yet.** `core_problems.md` lists no open questions or pending follow-ups for this issue ("Por enquanto sem" for both). This is a lighter discovery than EX Account Health: the problem, root cause, and correct process are already known — what's missing is likely *why* the correct process isn't being followed consistently (a Step 3+ discovery question), not *what* the correct process is.

**Confirmed (08/06/2026):** this is **not** a data product. An earlier judgment call (07/30/2026, logged in the EX Account Health scratchpad) had already kept this issue out of that PRD for the same reason. The fix here is a Salesforce configuration/automation change (validation rule, auto-fill Flow, and/or a proactive Slack alert — see "Possible Products" below), not a new product to design and build.

## Possible Products — Solution Space Being Explored

Still collecting possibilities — no direction chosen yet. Three concrete technical ideas from Lorena, explored below against Salesforce's own documentation:

1. **Hard block:** don't allow the deal to be marked Won at all while the "Opportunity Team (Commissioned)" field is blank.
2. **Auto-fill:** populate that field automatically by pulling the names from somewhere else in Salesforce, instead of relying on manual entry.
3. **Proactive Slack alert (earlier layer, while the deal is still open):** before the deal even reaches Won, send a Slack alert to whoever owns filling in the "Sales Architect" fields, warning that they're still blank — so the person fixes it early instead of only being stopped at the moment of closing.
4. **Gate the handoff email instead of the deal:** leave the Won action alone entirely — instead, hold/delay the automated commission handoff email to Finance until the required fields are confirmed filled, decoupling "closing the deal" (the AE's action) from "notifying Finance" (which depends on the CD's data). Surfaced after learning the real bottleneck is a one-time notification fired at the moment of Won, not an inability to correct the deal afterward. Beatriz thinks this is probably feasible, but flagged a new risk: Operations depends on these emails for internal allocation processes, and delaying them could affect that (see "Discovery Answers" above, and R3 below).
5. **Alert on post-close changes (Beatriz's own idea, 08/07/2026):** whenever a closed deal's data is edited afterward, fire an alert instead of relying on people informally telling Beatriz. Not mutually exclusive with idea 4 — could work as a safety net on its own, or as a complement if idea 4 turns out to have trade-offs for Operations.

**Revised problem framing (08/07/2026):** the original framing — "can't correct retroactively" — was imprecise. Beatriz confirmed a Won deal *can* be reopened/edited; what actually fails to reach Finance is the one-time automated handoff email, snapshotted at the moment of Won. So the fix needs to happen either before the Won click (ideas 1–3) or by changing when/how that notification fires (idea 4) — not necessarily both. Ideas aren't mutually exclusive: idea 3 is a softer, earlier nudge; idea 1 acts at the closing moment; idea 4 acts at the notification moment instead of the closing moment; idea 2 removes the need for anyone to act at all, if a reliable source exists.

### Technical Exploration — Salesforce Validation Rule / Auto-fill Options (08/06/2026)

**Confirmed structure (screenshot review, 08/06/2026 — structure only, no client names kept here):** the "Opportunity Team (Commissioned)" section, on the "Sales Architect" tab of the Opportunity, is **not** the generic related-list feature originally assumed. It's three separate, individually-labeled fields:
- **Consulting Director** — required.
- **Solution Architect** — required.
- **Other Experts** — optional, and appears to be a free-text field that can hold more than one person's name in the same field (no separate row per person).

This is simpler than the standard Salesforce "Opportunity Team" feature, and changes both ideas below.

**On the hard-block idea (validation rule):**
- Because these are plain fields (not related-list child records), the earlier concern about validation rules being unable to "see" the data doesn't apply here. A standard validation rule can check each field directly with `ISBLANK()` — no helper counter field or extra Flow needed.
- Since "Other Experts" isn't always required, the rule would only need to cover the two required fields: block the save when the deal is being marked Won **and** ("Consulting Director" is blank **or** "Solution Architect" is blank). "Other Experts" stays outside the rule, exactly as optional as it is today.

**On the auto-fill idea:**
- Whether "Consulting Director" and "Solution Architect" can be auto-filled still depends on whether a reliable source exists elsewhere in Salesforce that already knows who these people are for a given deal (open question below) — that part is unchanged.
- "Other Experts" is a worse candidate for auto-fill than originally thought: being free text that can hold multiple names with no defined structure, there's no reliable way for an automation to know how many people belong there or how to format them. Auto-fill, if pursued at all, most likely only makes sense for the two required fields — "Other Experts" would stay manual.

**Working read:** the hard-block idea got noticeably simpler once the real field structure was confirmed — it's a single, standard validation rule, no extra automation required. It also has a real precedent, not just a hypothesis: PP confirmed a similar block already existed and worked when the CRM was Bitrix, before the migration to Salesforce (see "Discovery Answers" above) — reinforcing that this isn't a new, untested idea for Indicium. **However**, Beatriz (Salesforce owner) raised a real concern about *where* a Won-stage block lands: it would stop the AE, not the CD/SA who's actually responsible for the data — risking fabricated placeholder data as a workaround instead of a real fix (see "Discovery Answers" above). This doesn't rule the idea out, but means the enforcement point needs rethinking — e.g., blocking/flagging at the CD/SA level specifically, rather than at the AE's Won action. The auto-fill idea is still only partially promising (the two required fields, not "Other Experts"), and still depends on confirming a trustworthy data source to copy from. Auto-fill is worth weighing more heavily now, since — if it worked — it would sidestep the actor-mismatch problem entirely (no one would need to be blocked at all).

### Technical Exploration — Proactive Slack Alert (08/06/2026)

**The trigger can come entirely from data already in Salesforce — no new external data needed.** The condition "deal is open, not yet Won/Lost, and 'Consulting Director' or 'Solution Architect' is still blank" is something a **Schedule-Triggered Flow** (a Flow that runs on its own schedule — e.g., once a day) can check directly, since every piece of that condition (stage, blank fields, days since created/last modified) already lives on the Opportunity record. So this doesn't need a new "field is empty" trigger to be invented — it reuses the exact same fields the validation-rule idea checks, just on a timer instead of at the moment of saving.

**Three ways to actually deliver the Slack message, in order of setup effort:**
1. **"Sales Cloud for Slack" (Salesforce's own official app).** If this app is already installed and connected in Indicium's org, it adds ready-made Flow actions (e.g., "Send Slack notification") — the Flow just picks a message destination and record, no custom webhook plumbing needed. This is the simplest path *if* the app is already there — unconfirmed (see open questions).
2. **A Slack Flow action from a managed package** (e.g., a "Slack Actions" package some orgs install). Similar ease of use to option 1, but depends on a specific package being installed rather than Salesforce's own native app.
3. **A custom webhook call, built by hand.** Create a Slack Incoming Webhook (a URL Slack gives you that posts to a specific channel), store it securely in Salesforce as a Named Credential (so it's not hardcoded), and call it from the Flow (via an HTTP callout/External Service action, or a small Apex action invoked by the Flow). More setup work, but doesn't depend on any particular app being installed — works regardless of what's already in the org.

**Working read:** the schedule + condition part is straightforward either way (reuses existing Opportunity data, no new tracking needed). The only real fork is *how* the message reaches Slack — worth checking first whether option 1 (native app) is already active in Indicium's org, since that would make this by far the easiest of the three ideas to actually build.

**A fourth option for the "how it's delivered" fork, found via PP's own artifact (08/06/2026):** the Slack alert doesn't have to carry the gap data itself — it could just be a short message with a **link to a live Claude-artifact report** (the same mechanism behind PP's personal Friday artifact). This sidesteps the whole "which Salesforce-to-Slack integration" question above, at the cost of depending on Claude artifacts as a delivery channel instead of a Slack-native one — see "Connection to EX Account Health" above and the related open questions below.

## Proposed Solution — Sent to Beatriz for Validation (08/11/2026)

Lorena aligned a concrete rollout plan with the CDO (08/10/2026) and sent it directly to Beatriz on Slack the next morning (08/11/2026) — **not yet answered by Beatriz as of this writing.** The plan combines several of the ideas above into a phased sequence, starting with the lightest-touch option and escalating only if needed:

1. **Meeting with all CDs** to explain the process and the impact of leaving these fields blank — this is Beatriz's own planned alignment call (see open question 16), now explicitly framed as step 1 of the rollout, not a separate parallel track.
2. **Configure two separate triggers for the deal-closing email — one for Ops, one for Finance.** This is a refinement of idea 4 (gate the handoff email): instead of one single email serving both audiences, split it into two independently-triggerable notifications. This directly addresses R3 (Ops's dependency on the existing email) by letting Ops's trigger stay as-is (fire at Won, unconditioned) while only Finance's trigger gets gated on the required fields being filled — sidestepping the Ops-impact risk instead of resolving it as a shared constraint.
3. **Slack notification to the CD** when the required fields still aren't filled in — this is idea 3 (proactive Slack alert), scoped specifically to notifying the CD.
4. **Slack notification to Beatriz** if the people listed in the commissioned fields are changed *after* the email has already been sent to Finance — a narrower version of idea 5 (alert on post-close changes), scoped specifically to changes affecting the commissioned-fields data, not any change to a closed deal.

**Sequencing — deliberately incremental:** Lorena's proposal to Beatriz is to start with only step 1 (the meeting), then monitor its actual impact before deciding whether the remaining technical layers (2–4) are still needed. This treats the people/alignment fix as the first, cheapest intervention and the technical guardrails as escalation only if alignment alone doesn't hold — consistent with Beatriz's own original read that this might be more of a people problem than a system problem.

**Not yet validated:** this is a proposal awaiting Beatriz's response, not a confirmed direction. In particular, step 2 (splitting the email trigger in two) is a new technical detail not yet checked against Beatriz's "acho que dá para fazer isso sim" read on idea 4 — it's a more specific ask than what she was originally asked to assess, so her technical read may change once she sees this exact split.
