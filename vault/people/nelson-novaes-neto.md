# Nelson Novaes Neto

> CTO and Partner at C6 Bank, founding member of the bank's technology team since 2018. Research Affiliate at MIT Sloan (Cybersecurity at MIT Sloan / CAMS) and co-author of a published academic framework on AI security — one of the key stakeholders for [[ai-transformation-project-c6-bank]], alongside [[paulo-pituba]].

## Team

No internal team — Nelson Novaes Neto is an external stakeholder at C6 Bank, client side of [[ai-transformation-project-c6-bank]].

## Background

- Education: telecommunications engineering; postgraduate in information security (USP); MBA (FGV); Master's in Experimental Psychology (PUC-SP, ~2011) — an unusual combination that explains his interest in organizational culture and human behavior applied to security/technology, not just technical architecture.
- Career path: Superintendent at Itaú Unibanco → CSO at Grupo UOL (over a decade, trained 20,000+ professionals) → CTO & Partner at C6 Bank (since 2018, founding tech team).
- Since 2019: Research Affiliate at MIT Sloan School of Management, in Stuart Madnick's cybersecurity lab (Cybersecurity at MIT Sloan / CAMS).
- Board member, (ISC)² Latin America; agenda contributor, World Economic Forum; recurring speaker at RSA Conference and PCI Security Standards Council.
- One of the architects of C6's founding culture (6 ideals, "Csixer" identity) alongside CISO José Santana — notably, the first person he hired as CTO was from the security team, signaling security was foundational, not an afterthought.

## Academic work (key context for the project)

Co-author of "An Executive Guide to Secure-by-Design AI" (2025, with Keri Pearlson/MIT) — see [[secure-by-design-ai-framework]]. His research lineage before AI: large-scale data breach analysis and human behavior under security controls (Capital One Data Breach case study, 2020; Global Data Breach Database, 2021; with Stuart Madnick and MIT CISL/CAMS collaborators). His master's thesis in experimental psychology (behaviorism) — see [[aversive-stimulus-security-controls]] — is the direct intellectual root of his AI security framework.

MIT and JISSEC sources claim the Secure-by-Design AI framework was piloted at C6 Bank (a "four-part platform architecture" separating experimentation from production, with "19 critical design considerations") — but this is self-reported (Nelson is both co-author and CTO) with no independent confirmation, and neither the four parts nor the 19 considerations are enumerated in accessible sources. Treat as a well-founded hypothesis, not a confirmed fact — worth asking him directly.

## Current AI initiatives at C6 (separate, operational context)

C6 already runs generative AI in production with AWS on at least two use cases: internal source-code review and customer service (Q&A). This is not an early-exploration phase — the project's angle is likely to scale, govern, and structure what already exists, not "convince the bank to start with AI."

## Business focus

Internal notes (2026-09-01) confirm the pattern already visible in his public profile: he is more technical, a cybersecurity reference, focused on how to guarantee AI projects are secure — and tends to view consulting engagements more as operational support, in contrast with [[paulo-pituba]]'s business/results-oriented focus.

## Rapport approach

Apply the [[never-split-the-difference-negotiation-tactics]] playbook — especially: use his own academic framework as shared vocabulary instead of proposing a competing methodology, lead with calibrated questions about how the 10-question framework is used day-to-day, and use mountaineering/expedition analogies (he has climbed Elbrus, Kilimanjaro, Mont Blanc, Aconcágua, and Gran Paradiso) rather than sports/military metaphors.

Vocabulary that resonates: "secure-by-design", "governance before innovation", "calculated risk", "trust and transparency with the customer". Avoid unsupported hype language ("revolutionary", "disruptive") — he responds better to rigor and evidence than to enthusiasm.

Internal team notes from the project kickoff (see [[2026-09-08-c6-ai-transformation-kickoff-interno]]) narrow his scope: referred to internally as "Nelson," he runs Corebank day-to-day technical operations — distinct from [[paulo-pituba]]'s transformation-program focus — and is the sponsor of the [[2026-09-knowledge-works-c6]] workstream (reducing Shadow AI / ungoverned generative-AI use in business areas).
