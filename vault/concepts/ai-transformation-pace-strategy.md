# AI Transformation Pace Strategy

> Each organization evolves People, Processes, and Products at its own pace, according to its business capabilities and priorities.

## Description

A strategic choice of how aggressively an organization advances People, Processes, and Products together in an AI transformation. Three pace options are offered: [[simultaneous-transformation]] (all three axes advance in parallel from the start), [[sequenced-transformation]] (People and Processes first, then Products), and [[incremental-transformation]] (one domain at a time, expanding as maturity grows).

## Key Characteristics

- Frames pace as a deliberate strategic choice, not a default
- Three named options, each suited to a different risk/sponsorship profile
- Directly informs the effort distribution described in the [[ai-maturity-journey]]

## Where it Applies

- Presented in the ROI/strategy section of Indicium's Claude Cowork enablement workshops (e.g., Portobank, Aug 2026) to help leadership choose a rollout pace.

## Related Concepts

- [[incremental-transformation]] — the gradual/one-domain-at-a-time pace option
- [[ai-transformation-office]] — the structure this strategy governs the rollout of
- [[ai-maturity-journey]] — how effort allocation shifts once a pace is chosen
