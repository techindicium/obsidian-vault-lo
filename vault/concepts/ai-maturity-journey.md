# AI Maturity Journey

> Early on, focus stays on People: enabling Claude Cowork and Code users. As maturity rises, effort migrates to Processes and Products.

## Description

A model describing how program effort is distributed across the three axes (People, Processes, Products) as an organization's AI adoption matures. In an illustrative four-phase breakdown, effort starts heavily weighted toward People (85%/15%/0%) and gradually shifts toward Processes and Products (15%/35%/50% by the final phase).

## Key Characteristics

- Effort starts concentrated on People (enabling users) in early phases
- Shifts progressively toward Processes, then Products, as maturity increases
- Illustrative phase breakdown: 85/15/0 → 50/35/15 → 35/40/25 → 15/35/50 (People/Processes/Products)

## Where it Applies

- Presented as the maturity curve underlying the [[ai-transformation-pace-strategy]] choice in Indicium's Claude Cowork enablement workshops (e.g., Portobank, Aug 2026).
