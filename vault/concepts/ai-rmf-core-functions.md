# AI RMF Core Functions

> The four functions that make up the operational core of the [[nist-ai-risk-management-framework|NIST AI RMF]]: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE.

## Description

The AI RMF Core organizes AI risk management activities into four functions. GOVERN cuts across and informs the other three; MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE are applied within the context GOVERN establishes, and the four are meant to be applied continuously and iteratively — not as a single linear pass — throughout the [[ai-lifecycle-dimensions|AI lifecycle]].

## The Four Functions

### GOVERN
Cultivates and implements a culture of risk management within an organization: establishing policies, processes, procedures, and practices across the organization related to the mapping, measuring, and managing of AI risks. GOVERN is cross-cutting — it informs and is infused throughout the other three functions.

### MAP
Establishes the context to frame risks related to an AI system: understanding the system's intended purpose, use cases, stakeholders, and how it fits into the broader lifecycle, so that risks can be identified before they are measured or managed.

### MEASURE
Employs quantitative, qualitative, or mixed-method tools, techniques, and methodologies to analyze, assess, benchmark, and monitor AI risk and its associated impacts — directly grappling with the challenges of [[ai-rmf-risk-framing-challenges|risk measurement]].

### MANAGE
Allocates risk-management resources to mapped and measured risks on a regular basis and as defined by the GOVERN function — prioritizing and acting on risk responses (e.g., mitigate, transfer, avoid, accept) based on [[ai-rmf-risk-framing-challenges|risk tolerance and prioritization]].
