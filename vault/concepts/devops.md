# DevOps

> Practices that combine development and operations to shorten the software delivery lifecycle and enable continuous delivery.

## Description

DevOps teams are called out as one of the primary adopters of [[shift-left-testing|shift-left testing]] — the campaign to move critical testing practices earlier in the lifecycle is framed explicitly as an agile/DevOps initiative. This vault entry captures DevOps in that role as introduced by the shift-left testing literature; a fuller treatment belongs to dedicated DevOps/SDLC sources as they are ingested.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines development and operations practices to shorten lead time and enable continuous delivery.
- Closely associated with adopting [[shift-left-testing|shift-left testing]] and [[continuous-testing|continuous testing]].

## Where it Applies

- [[shift-left-testing]] — DevOps teams are named as a primary driver of shift-left adoption
