# Simultaneous Transformation (Aggressive Pace)

> People, Processes, and Products advance in parallel from the start.

## Description

One of the three options in the [[ai-transformation-pace-strategy]]: all three transformation axes are pursued at the same time from day one. Recommended for organizations with strong executive sponsorship, high competitive pressure, and willingness to absorb simultaneous change across the business.

## Key Characteristics

- All three axes (People, Processes, Products) move together from the start
- Requires strong, sustained executive sponsorship
- Suited to high competitive-pressure situations where speed outweighs sequencing risk

## Where it Applies

- One of three pace options presented in Indicium's Claude Cowork enablement workshops (e.g., Portobank, Aug 2026).
