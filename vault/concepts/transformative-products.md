# Transformative Products (Product Value Axis)

> AI embedded in the product's own behavior and evolution.

## Description

The third of the [[three-value-axes-ai]]: embedding AI natively into a product's behavior rather than adding it as a feature on the side, so the product itself evolves around AI. Cited impact in workshop material: 200% more end-customer reach in the same period.

## Key Characteristics

- AI is embedded in the product's core behavior, not a bolt-on feature
- Reported impact: 200% increase in end-customer reach (cited example)
- The final, compounding layer on top of [[smarter-employees]] and [[faster-processes]]

## Where it Applies

- Third value axis presented in Indicium's Claude Cowork enablement workshops (e.g., Portobank, Aug 2026), paired with the [[core-innovation]] pillar.
