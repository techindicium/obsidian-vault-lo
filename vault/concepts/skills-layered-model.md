# Layered Skills Model (You / Team / Organization)

> Give Cowork your team's best practices; give Cowork the organization's best practices.

## Description

Skills adoption scales in three layers. At the individual layer, a person personalizes Skills with their own instructions and golden examples, transferring their personal expertise (e.g., a meeting-prep or email-draft Skill). At the team layer, the team turns Cowork into a shared "chief of staff" by encoding the team's best practices (e.g., a portfolio-analysis or credit-opinion Skill). At the organization layer, the whole company's standards and brand are encoded (e.g., a company-slides or regulatory-summary Skill).

## Key Characteristics

- Individual layer: personal productivity Skills built from one person's own workflow
- Team layer: shared Skills encoding a team's specific best practices
- Organization layer: company-wide Skills encoding brand and compliance standards

## Where it Applies

- Presented as the adoption ladder for Skills in Indicium's Claude Cowork enablement workshops (e.g., Portobank, Aug 2026).
