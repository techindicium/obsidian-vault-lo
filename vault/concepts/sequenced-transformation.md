# Sequenced Transformation (Balanced Pace)

> Starts with People and Processes, consolidating adoption and gains before expanding to Products.

## Description

One of the three options in the [[ai-transformation-pace-strategy]]: People and Processes are transformed first, and gains are consolidated before expanding into Products. Suited to organizations seeking to scale results in a structured way, balancing speed, adoption, and risk.

## Key Characteristics

- People and Processes are prioritized before Products
- Gains are consolidated at each stage before expanding scope
- Balances speed of results against adoption risk

## Where it Applies

- One of three pace options presented in Indicium's Claude Cowork enablement workshops (e.g., Portobank, Aug 2026).
