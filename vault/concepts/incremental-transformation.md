# Incremental Transformation (Gradual Pace)

> Prioritizes one domain at a time and expands as maturity grows.

## Description

One of the three options in the [[ai-transformation-pace-strategy]]: the organization prioritizes a single domain, proves value, and only then expands into the next. Suited to organizations that prefer to validate value before increasing investment.

## Key Characteristics

- One domain at a time, expanding only after proving value
- Lowest execution risk of the three pace options
- Suited to organizations cautious about upfront investment

## Where it Applies

- One of three pace options presented in Indicium's Claude Cowork enablement workshops (e.g., Portobank, Aug 2026).
