# Secure-by-Design AI Framework

> A framework co-authored by [[nelson-novaes-neto]] and Keri Pearlson (MIT Sloan), published in the *Journal of Information System Security* (2025) as "An Executive Guide to Secure-by-Design AI", condensing AI system security into 10 strategic questions for executives.

## The 10 questions

1. Strategic alignment — how AI aligns with the organization's objectives, budget, values, and ethics
2. Risk management — how to identify, assess, and prioritize AI-specific risks
3. Control implementation — which controls/tools mitigate the risks
4. Policies, standards, and procedures — data quality, privacy, ethics, security
5. Governance structure — who oversees development, deployment, security, and operations
6. Technical feasibility — compatibility of the AI architecture with existing infrastructure
7. Resource allocation — security effort required and ownership
8. Performance and security monitoring — effectiveness and security metrics
9. Continuous improvement — monitoring and adaptation
10. Stakeholder engagement — how to communicate security, privacy, and ethics to build shared accountability

## Reported application at C6 Bank (limited confidence)

MIT Sloan and the JISSEC paper claim the framework was applied at C6 Bank, resulting in a "four-part platform architecture" separating AI experimentation from production, and surfacing "19 critical design considerations." Neither the four parts nor the 19 considerations are enumerated in any accessible source, and the only sources are MIT itself and the journal that published the paper — no independent press or C6 confirmation exists. Treat as a well-founded hypothesis to validate directly with [[nelson-novaes-neto]], not a confirmed fact — avoid citing the specific numbers as if already known.
