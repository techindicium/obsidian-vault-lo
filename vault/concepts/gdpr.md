# GDPR

> The EU's General Data Protection Regulation — a data protection and privacy law that creates compliance requirements for how software handles personal data.

## Description

GDPR is cited as an example of a regulatory driver for "secure by design" [[coding-standards|coding standards]]: building security into code from the beginning isn't just good practice, it's a legal requirement in contexts where regulations like GDPR apply. This vault entry captures GDPR only in that governance/compliance-driver role as introduced by the shift-left testing literature — a fuller treatment belongs to dedicated governance/compliance sources as they are ingested.

## Key Characteristics

- EU regulation governing personal data protection and privacy.
- Referenced here as a compliance driver requiring secure-by-design engineering practices.
