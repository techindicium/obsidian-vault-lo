# Faster Processes (Process Value Axis)

> Operations redesigned with AI in the loop.

## Description

The second of the [[three-value-axes-ai]]: redesigning operational processes with AI actively in the loop, rather than layering AI on top of an unchanged process. Cited unit-cost reduction in workshop material ranges from 30% to 60%.

## Key Characteristics

- Requires process redesign, not just tool adoption on top of existing steps
- Reported impact range: 30–60% unit-cost reduction
- Builds on gains from the [[smarter-employees]] axis and feeds the [[transformative-products]] axis

## Where it Applies

- Second value axis presented in Indicium's Claude Cowork enablement workshops (e.g., Portobank, Aug 2026), paired with the [[agentic-dev]] pillar.
