# Core Innovation (AI-Native Core Systems)

> Products mesh with AI-native solutions at the core of the business and redefine the customer experience.

## Description

Core Innovation is the pillar of an [[ai-transformation-office]] focused on the product layer: embedding AI-native solutions directly into an organization's core systems (e.g., a bank's core banking platform), rather than bolting AI on as a side feature, in order to redefine the end-customer experience.

## Key Characteristics

- Targets core, customer-facing systems rather than internal tooling
- AI is embedded natively into the product, not added as an add-on
- Maps to the "Transformative Products" value axis

## Where it Applies

- Positioned as the third of three AITO pillars (with Workplace Transformation and Agentic Dev) in Indicium's Claude Cowork enablement workshops (e.g., Portobank, Aug 2026).
