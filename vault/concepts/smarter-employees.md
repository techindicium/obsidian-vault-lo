# Smarter Employees (People Value Axis)

> AI in the daily work of every function.

## Description

The first of the [[three-value-axes-ai]]: embedding AI directly into each employee's day-to-day work across functions, rather than confining it to a single team. Cited productivity gains in workshop material range from 20% to 45%.

## Key Characteristics

- Scope: individual and team productivity across all functions, not one department
- Reported impact range: 20–45% productivity increase
- Feeds into the [[faster-processes]] axis once adoption matures into process redesign

## Where it Applies

- First value axis presented in Indicium's Claude Cowork enablement workshops (e.g., Portobank, Aug 2026), paired with the [[workplace-transformation]] pillar.

- [[three-value-axes-ai]] — the framework this axis belongs to
