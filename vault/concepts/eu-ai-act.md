# EU AI Act

> The European Union's binding legal regulation governing AI systems — unlike voluntary frameworks such as the NIST AI RMF or certifiable-but-optional standards like ISO/IEC 42001, this one carries the force of law within the EU.

## Description

Captured here as a lightweight reference stub because [[iso-42001|ISO/IEC 42001]] is discussed in relation to it: as of 2026, ISO 42001 is not a "harmonized standard" under the EU AI Act, so certification does not grant an automatic "presumption of conformity" — but it does provide strong, certifiable evidence of the AI governance practices the Act requires. ## Where it Applies
