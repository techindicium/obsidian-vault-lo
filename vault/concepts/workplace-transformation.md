# Workplace Transformation

> The engine starts from People: teams adopt AI and convert productivity into new processes.

## Description

Workplace Transformation is the pillar of an [[ai-transformation-office]] focused on collaboration and Cowork adoption. It is the entry point of the transformation engine: as teams adopt AI collaboration tools in their daily work, the productivity gained is then converted into redesigned processes, feeding the other two axes.

## Key Characteristics

- Entry-point pillar: transformation typically starts here before expanding
- Converts individual/team AI adoption into process-level change
- Directly maps to the "Smarter Employees" value axis

## Where it Applies

- Positioned as the first of three AITO pillars (with Agentic Dev and Core Innovation) in Indicium's Claude Cowork enablement workshops (e.g., Portobank, Aug 2026).
